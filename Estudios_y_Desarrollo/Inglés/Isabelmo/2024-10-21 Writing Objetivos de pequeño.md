## Cuáles fueron tus objetivos de pequeño y si todavía se cumplen o se han cumplido ya. Which were your objectives when you were younger and if those objectives have changed. What made you change that thought (provided you have changed your mind about that)
Since I was just a child, I have been a person with big dreams. 

I remember myself watching the F1 with my uncle in Almonte. I loved thinking about being one of the drivers of the race, with an hiper fast car at 300km/h. Today, of course, I am not a F1 driver, but I really love all what is related to cars. I go karting so often, each two weeks more or less. When I sit on the kart, I feel like the driver of F1 and it is felt like my child dream.

When I was a little older, my dreams changed to more affordable  ones. For example, I started riding the bike at thirteen years old, and usally came the 6-7th of the grid. I worked hard to improve and to win a race. After some years, I could not only win a race but a championship also.

I think this kind of goals has made me the person I am today nowadays. Of course my goals and my dreams have changed, but I think all of them, the victorys, the fails, they have taught me a lot.
