analyzer = machine 

  

ADJETIVOS

  

Example: the computer was so old that it did not work properly. (El ordenador era tan antiguo que no funcionaba correctamente). 

  

La estructura es SO + ADJ + THAT. NO añadimos ER / EST al final del adjetivo. 

  

Example: Your computer was older than mine. (Tu ordenador era más antiguo que el mío). SÍ añadimos ER puesto que estamos comparando dos objetos.

  

Example: Your computer was the fastest I have ever worked with. (Tu ordenador era el más rápido con el que yo he trabajado). SÍ añadimos EST puesto que se trata de un superlativo. 

  

PRESENT PERFECT / PAST SIMPLE 

  

Usamos Present Perfect cuando la acción sigue teniendo consecuencia en el presente. 

Example: I have worked for this company for many years. (Trabajo para esta empresa desde hace muchos años). 

  

Usamos Past Simple cuando la acción tuvo lugar en un pasado que ya ha acabado. 

Example: I studied IT at the university many years ago. (Estudié informática en la universidad hace muchos años.

Since I was just a child, I have been a person with big dreams. 

  

I remember watching F1 with my uncle in Almonte. I loved thinking about being one of the drivers of the race, with an hiper fast car at 300km/h. Today, of course, I am not an F1 driver, but I really love everything related to cars. I go karting so often, each two weeks more or less. When I sit on the kart, I feel like the driver of F1 and it feels like my child's dream.

  

When I was a little older, my dreams changed to more affordable  ones. For example, I started riding the bike at thirteen years old, and usually came 6-7th of the grid. I worked hard to improve and to win a race. After some years, I could not only win a race but a championship also/too.

  

I think this kind of goal has made me the person I am today. Of course my goals and my dreams have changed, but I think all of them, the victories and the failures have taught me a lot.
